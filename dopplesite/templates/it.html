For IT teams

BRB at a glance

Someone at your organization would like to use BRB. This page sets out what it is, what it installs, what it sends over the network, and what it does on the computer, so you can review it quickly.

What it is
A desktop app that lets a person step away from a video meeting for a moment without their camera going dark: it switches their meeting camera between their live webcam and a short recorded loop of them.
Publisher
Happy Hacks (happyhacks.io), a brand of Busy Beaver Group LLC · support@happyhacks.io
Platforms
Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
How it reaches meetings
As a camera: Windows: its own HappyHacks Virtual Camera; Mac: OBS Studio's virtual camera. Works with any meeting app that lets you choose a camera (Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Zoom and others). No plug-ins, no meeting-app integrations.
Video and audio
Never leave the computer. The webcam picture, recorded loops and the person-detection AI all stay and run locally. BRB doesn't record, process or send audio.
Account
None needed to use it. A free allowance of LOOP time per computer; unlimited use is an individual subscription bought on this site.
Terms
End User License Agreement · Privacy Policy

Data: what's collected, and what isn't

BRB collects no personal or company data beyond what's needed to create and manage an account and its license. Nothing is sold or shared.

  • For licensing (sent by the app): a scrambled device ID (a SHA-256 of the computer's hardware ID; the hardware ID itself never leaves the computer), the computer's name, the operating system, the BRB version, the license key if there is one, and the internet address the check comes from (used to prevent abuse of the free allowance).
  • For an account (only if the person signs in or subscribes on this website): their name and email address, through a Happy Hacks account. Card details go to Square, the payment processor, and never reach us.

Never collected: video, images, audio, keystrokes, files, meeting content or participants, contacts, calendars, browsing activity, location, or information about the organization, its network or other users. No analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.

Full details, retention and contact: Privacy Policy.

What it's for (and not for)

BRB is a courtesy tool for short, occasional breaks - a restroom break, a coffee refill, answering the door - in meetings where stepping away briefly is fine. Its EULA prohibits using it to misrepresent attendance or participation, to get around attendance or identity checks or proctoring, or in exams, interviews or any meeting where live presence is required, and requires users to follow their organization's policies.

Users can turn on an optional BRB badge ("BRB · Be right back", bottom-right of their video) that tells others they've stepped away while the loop is on air.
