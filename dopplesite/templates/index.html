Installing on Windows
  1. Open BRBSetup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  2. The installer adds the HappyHacks Virtual Camera (Windows asks for permission once); the setup guide helps you pick your webcam and record your first loop.
  3. In your meeting app's video settings, choose HappyHacks Virtual Camera as your camera.
Installing on a Mac
  1. Open the .dmg and drag BRB onto Applications.
  2. Open BRB. The first time, macOS says it "can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" (the app isn't notarized by Apple yet). Click OK, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Open it again and choose Open. You only do this once.
  3. Install OBS Studio (free). BRB uses its virtual camera; the setup guide walks you through it, and through allowing the camera and hotkeys.
  4. In your meeting app's video settings, choose OBS Virtual Camera.

What it does

Like you never left

A natural, seamless loop of you stands in while you're away.

One key, either way

Tap F9 as you step away and again when you're back. Hold it, and BRB switches the moment you're lined up.

Back in the same spot

Outlines of you and the loop show where to sit, so coming back is smooth too.

Upfront when you want

The optional BRB badge tells others you've stepped away while your loop is on air.

Quiet while you're away

Your microphone is muted while the loop plays, so a coffee grinder or a barking dog never reaches the meeting.

Made for short breaks

BRB is a courtesy tool: it saves everyone the distraction of a camera switching off, an empty chair or a kitchen in the background while you take a moment. Use it for brief breaks in meetings, classes and trainings where stepping away is fine.

It is not for faking attendance or participation, or for getting around attendance checks, identity checks, proctored exams, interviews or any meeting where your live presence is required. Follow your organizer's, employer's or school's rules; when in doubt, simply turn your camera off. Read the EULA.

FAQs

Which meeting apps does BRB work with?

Any video meeting app that lets you choose a camera, including Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex and Zoom. BRB appears as a camera (HappyHacks Virtual Camera on Windows, OBS Virtual Camera on a Mac); pick it in your meeting app's video settings.

Is my video uploaded anywhere? What data does BRB collect?

Your video never leaves your computer: your camera, your loops and your recordings stay on it. BRB collects no personal or company data beyond what's needed for your account and license: for license checks, a scrambled device ID, your computer's name, operating system and BRB version; and, if you subscribe, your name and email. Nothing is sold or shared. Read the Privacy Policy.

Why does it need an internet connection?

Free LOOP time and license keys are checked with brb.happyhacks.io while the LOOP is on air, so reinstalling doesn't reset free time. Your live camera works without a connection.

How long can the loop be?

You choose: from 1 to 10 minutes (90 seconds by default). Longer loops look more natural for longer breaks.

Can people tell when I've stepped away?

Only if you want them to. Turn on the BRB Watermark switch and a "BRB · Be right back" watermark appears on your video while you're away (drag it where you like; on you, it stays visible even with a virtual background), then disappears when you're back. It's off by default; use it if your organizer asks for it, or if you simply prefer to let people know.

Is it OK to use BRB in my meeting?

BRB is meant for short, courteous breaks where stepping away is fine. Don't use it to fake attendance or in exams, interviews or meetings that require your live presence. If you're unsure, ask the organizer or turn your camera off.

My company's IT needs to approve software. What do I send them?

Send them our page for IT teams: what BRB installs, what it sends over the network (no video or audio ever leaves your computer), permissions and file checksums, all in one place. It has a button that starts the email for you.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Any time, on the license page. Your key keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.
