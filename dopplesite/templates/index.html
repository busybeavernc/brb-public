Installing on Windows
  1. Open DopplegangerSetup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  2. The installer adds the HappyHacks Virtual Camera (Windows asks for permission once); the setup guide helps you pick your webcam and record your first loop.
  3. In your meeting app's video settings, choose HappyHacks Virtual Camera as your camera.
Installing on a Mac
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Doppleganger onto Applications.
  2. Open Doppleganger. The first time, macOS says it "can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" (the app isn't notarized by Apple yet). Click OK, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Open it again and choose Open. You only do this once.
  3. Install OBS Studio (free). Doppleganger uses its virtual camera; the setup guide walks you through it, and through allowing the camera and hotkeys.
  4. In your meeting app's video settings, choose OBS Virtual Camera.

What it does

A calm stand-in

Record a minute or two of yourself listening. Doppleganger joins the end to the start so the loop plays smoothly, without a jump.

One key, either way

Tap F9 as you step away and again when you're back. Hold it, and Doppleganger switches the moment you're lined up.

Back in the same spot

Outlines of you and the loop show where to sit, so coming back is smooth too.

Quiet while you're away

Your microphone is muted while the loop plays, so a coffee grinder or a barking dog never reaches the meeting.

Made for short breaks

Doppleganger is a courtesy tool: it saves everyone the distraction of a camera switching off, an empty chair or a kitchen in the background while you take a moment. Use it for brief breaks in meetings, classes and trainings where stepping away is fine.

It is not for faking attendance or participation, or for getting around attendance checks, identity checks, proctored exams, interviews or any meeting where your live presence is required. Follow your organizer's, employer's or school's rules; when in doubt, simply turn your camera off. Read the EULA.
