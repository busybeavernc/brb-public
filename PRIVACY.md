# BRB (Be Right Back) Privacy Policy

**Effective November 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Busy Beaver Group LLC, doing business as Happy Hacks ("Happy Hacks", "we", "us"), collects when you use BRB (Be Right Back), the desktop app (the "Software"), and the website brb.happyhacks.io (the "Website"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

**The short version:** BRB collects no personal or company data beyond what's needed to create and manage your account and your license. Your video, your loops and your recordings never leave your computer. We don't sell or rent your information, we don't use it for advertising, and we share it only with the few service providers that help us run BRB (listed below).

## 1. What stays on your computer

BRB works on your computer. The following are created and kept **only on your computer** and are never sent to us or anyone else:

- your webcam picture, and the video your meeting app receives from BRB;
- the loops you record, and their thumbnails;
- BRB's settings, its calibration pictures of your room, and its log files;
- the results of BRB's person detection, which runs entirely on your computer.

BRB does not record, process or send audio. While the loop is on air it can mute your microphone (you can turn this off); it only switches the microphone off and on again.

BRB listens for its own two hotkeys (F9 and F10) so they work while your meeting app is in front. It acts only on those keys. **It does not record, store or send your keystrokes.**

We cannot see or access anything stored on your computer.

## 2. What the Software sends to us (licensing)

To give each computer its free allowance and to check license keys, the Software contacts brb.happyhacks.io when it starts, every few minutes while it's open, and about every 15 seconds while the loop is on air. Each time, it sends:

- **a scrambled device identifier**: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your computer's hardware identifier. The hardware identifier itself never leaves your computer, and the hash can't be turned back into it;
- **your computer's name**, its **operating system** and the **BRB version**;
- **your license key**, if you have entered one.

Our server also sees the **internet (IP) address** the request comes from. We keep the address a computer was first seen from and the one it was last seen from, to prevent abuse of the free allowance (for example, limiting how many new computers can start from one internet connection in a day).

From these requests we keep, for each computer: the scrambled identifier, the computer name, operating system and BRB version, how much free LOOP time it has used, which license key (if any) it uses, when it was first and last seen, and the IP addresses described above. We also keep a record of when the loop was on air (start and end times), which is how free time is counted. We don't receive what you do while the loop is on air, which meetings you join, or who is in them.

## 3. Your account and purchases (only if you sign in or subscribe)

You don't need an account to download or use BRB. If you sign in on the Website or buy a subscription:

- **Sign-in** uses your Happy Hacks account. We receive and keep your **name**, **email address**, and **mobile number** if your Happy Hacks account has one, plus when your BRB account was created and when you last signed in.
- **Subscriptions** are paid through **Square**. You enter your payment details on Square's checkout page; **we never see or store your card details**. We keep your license key, its status and expiry, the Square subscription identifier, the price you subscribed at, and which computer the key is on.
- If you open the Website from the Software's "Get Unlimited Access Now" button, the link carries a signed reference to your computer's scrambled identifier, so a key you buy goes straight onto that computer.

## 4. The Website

- **Cookies.** We use only the cookies the Website needs to work: one that keeps you signed in and protects forms (it can also remember which computer opened the page, as described above; it lasts up to 30 days), and a short-lived one used only while you sign in. **No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.**
- **Downloads.** We count downloads per day, per platform and version, without recording who downloaded. The files themselves are delivered by GitHub (see below).
- **Server logs.** Like any web server, ours records requests (IP address, page requested, browser type and time) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are kept for a limited period.
- **No third-party scripts.** The Website doesn't load third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and its fonts are served from our own server.

## 5. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- provide BRB: count free LOOP time, check and apply license keys, and put purchased keys on the right computer;
- run subscriptions and billing, and answer your questions;
- prevent abuse and fraud, and keep the Software and the Website secure and working;
- comply with the law.

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising or profiling, or combine it with information from other sources to learn more about you.

## 6. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run BRB, and only what they need:

- **Square** processes subscription payments and sends your receipts ([Square's privacy notice](https://squareup.com/legal/general/privacy)).
- **GitHub** hosts BRB's downloads and updates. When you download BRB, or the Software checks for and downloads an update, your computer connects to GitHub, which receives your IP address ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)). The Software sends GitHub nothing about you.
- **Our hosting provider** runs the servers that store the information described here.
- **Happy Hacks sign-in** (happyhacks.io), operated by us, holds your Happy Hacks account.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, property or safety of Happy Hacks, our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Computer records and LOOP time records**: for as long as BRB offers a free allowance and licenses, because deleting them would reset a computer's free time or remove its key. You can ask us to delete yours (see section 9).
- **Account and license records**: for as long as your account or license exists, and afterwards for as long as needed for billing, tax and legal records.
- **Server logs**: for a limited period, then deleted.
- **Download counts**: kept as totals; they contain no personal information.

## 8. Security

All communication between the Software, the Website and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Computers are identified by a one-way hash, not their hardware identifier. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run BRB. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 9. Your choices and rights

- You can use the live camera without an internet connection; the Software contacts us only for the loop (licensing) and for updates.
- You can uninstall the Software at any time. Your loops and settings stay on your computer until you delete them.
- You can ask us to **see, correct or delete** the information we hold about you or your computer, or to close your account, by emailing **support@happyhacks.io**. Deleting a computer's record also deletes its free-time history, so its free allowance starts over; deleting an account doesn't cancel a Square subscription, which you cancel on your license page.
- Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law (for example, to object to or restrict processing, to receive your data in a portable form, or to complain to a data-protection authority). Contact us and we'll help.

We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in California law.

## 10. Children

BRB is not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 11. Where information is processed

Happy Hacks (Busy Beaver Group LLC) is based in the United States. Information may be stored and processed in the United States and wherever our service providers operate.

Please check this page from time to time; continuing to use the Software or the Website after a change means you accept the updated Policy.
